Voter ID — Forms Guide
Know which form to fill, what documents to carry, and how to apply online.

  
Form 6
New Voter Registration
Use this if you are enrolling for the first time
📄 Documents Required
Aadhaar CardPassport-size photoProof of age (Birth cert / 10th marksheet)Proof of residence
🖥️ How to apply online
  1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in and click "New Registration"
  2. Select Form 6 and fill in your personal details, date of birth and current address
  3. Upload a passport-size photo and your age & address proof documents
  4. Submit — you'll receive an application reference number by SMS/email
  5. A BLO (Booth Level Officer) may visit to verify your address
Apply via voters.eci.gov.in →

  
Form 8
Correction of Name, Date of Birth, Photo or Address
Fix any wrong or outdated information on your voter card
📄 Documents Required
Current Voter ID (EPIC)Aadhaar CardFor name: Aadhaar / PAN / Passport showing correct nameFor DOB: Birth certificate / School leaving certificate
🖥️ How to apply online
  1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in → Login or register
  2. Click "Correction of entries in Electoral Roll" → Form 8
  3. Search for your existing voter record using your EPIC number
  4. Select what you want to correct and upload supporting document
  5. Submit — note down the reference number
Apply via voters.eci.gov.in →

  
Form 8A
Shifting of Residence — Same Constituency
Moved to a new house but still in the same Assembly segment?
📄 Documents Required
Current Voter ID (EPIC)New address proof (Aadhaar / Utility bill / Rent agreement)
🖥️ How to apply online
  1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in and log in
  2. Choose "Shifting of residence" → Form 8A
  3. Enter your EPIC number to pull your existing record
  4. Enter the new address and upload address proof
  5. Submit — your polling booth will be updated to the nearest one
Apply via voters.eci.gov.in →

  
Form 7
Deletion / Objection to an Entry
To remove a deceased person's name or report a duplicate/wrong entry
📄 Documents Required
EPIC number of the person to be removedDeath certificate (for deceased persons)Relationship proof if filed by a family member
🖥️ How to apply online
  1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in → Login
  2. Select "Deletion of name" → Form 7
  3. Enter the EPIC number of the entry to be removed
  4. State the reason and upload proof
  5. Submit — the ERO reviews and processes the request
Apply via voters.eci.gov.in →

  
Form 6B
Link Aadhaar to Voter ID
Voluntary linking of Aadhaar with your EPIC to eliminate duplicate entries
📄 Documents Required
Aadhaar Card (or Aadhaar Enrolment Number)Your EPIC (Voter ID) number
🖥️ How to apply online
  1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in → "Link Aadhaar"
  2. Enter your EPIC number — your voter record will appear
  3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and submit
  4. An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile to confirm
  5. Once verified, your Aadhaar is linked — this is voluntary, not mandatory
Apply via voters.eci.gov.in →

  
ℹ️ Note
All forms are processed by the Electoral Registration Officer of your constituency. Processing typically takes 2–4 weeks. Track your application status using the reference number on voters.eci.gov.in.

What is SIR?
Special Intensive Revision

    
A plain-language guide to India's electoral roll revision process — what it is, its history, and how it affects every citizen's right to vote.

📋
What is it?
A special drive to update, correct and clean up the voter list — adding eligible new voters, removing deceased or shifted entries, and fixing errors.

    
📅
When does it happen?
The ECI announces SIR periodically — usually before major elections. A draft list is published, then claims and objections are invited from the public.

    
👤
Who conducts it?
The Election Commission of India (ECI) conducts SIR through local Electoral Registration Officers (EROs) and Booth Level Officers (BLOs) at the ground level.

    
🗳️
Why does it matter?
If your name is missing, wrong, or at the wrong polling booth — you cannot vote. SIR is your opportunity to fix that before the rolls are finalised.

  


  

    
Brief History of Electoral Roll Revision in India

    

      
1950
The Representation of the People Act, 1950 established the legal foundation for preparing and maintaining electoral rolls in India.

      
1950s – 1970s
Electoral rolls were prepared manually, house-to-house. Errors and omissions were common due to the scale and manual nature of the work.

      
1980s – 1990s
The ECI introduced structured revision cycles. Photo voter ID cards were introduced in 1993, replacing name-only entries.

      
2000s
Computerisation of electoral rolls began. The EPIC card became the standard. Online registration portals gradually replaced physical visits.

      
2015 onwards
NERPAP launched — linking EPIC with Aadhaar to eliminate duplicate entries. voters.eci.gov.in enabled fully digital registration and corrections.

      
2022 – Present
The ECI introduced quarterly roll updates (January, April, July, October) — giving citizens more windows to enrol or correct details throughout the year.

About the BLO Form

        
The BLO brings 2 identical printed forms to your home. Your existing voter details are already pre-printed on the form by the BLO. You only need to fill in the remaining blank fields — in Kannada or English, whichever you are comfortable with. Sign with date before handing it back.

      

    


    
    

      

        
1

        

          
Self Mapping

          
Your own details are present in the 2002 Electoral Roll

        

      

      

        The form has two columns at the top and a section below. In this case:

        ① Fill the left column only — enter your own details from the 2002 roll (your name, EPIC number, etc.) in that left column.

        ② Fill the section below — complete all the remaining fields at the bottom of the form (date of birth, mobile number, Aadhaar, etc.).

        Leave the right column blank. Sign with date at the bottom.
      

    


    
    

      

        
2

        

          
Progeny Mapping

          
You did not vote in 2002 or your details are not found — but a parent or grandparent's details are available

        

      

      

        In this case:

        ① Fill the right column — enter the details of the family member whose name appears in the 2002 roll (father, mother, grandfather or grandmother — whichever is available).

        ② Fill the section below — complete all the remaining fields at the bottom of the form (your own date of birth, mobile, Aadhaar, etc.).

        Leave the left column blank. Sign with date at the bottom.


        

          Which family member to use? Use whichever appears in the 2002 roll — father first, then mother, then grandfather, then grandmother. Only one is needed.
        

      

    


    
    

      

        
3

        

          
No 2002 Record Available

          
Neither your details nor any family member's details are found in the 2002 Electoral Roll

        

      

      

        In this case:

        ① Leave both the left and right columns completely blank.

        ② Fill only the section below — complete all the fields at the bottom of the form (your date of birth, mobile, Aadhaar, etc.).

        ③ Attach a birth proof document along with the form.


        

          
📎 Accepted birth proof documents:

          

            Birth Certificate
            School Transfer Certificate (TC)
            Marks Card (SSLC / 10th)

How SIR Directly Affects You

    

      
✅
Your vote is protected
A clean, accurate roll means your vote can only be cast by you. SIR removes ghost entries and duplicates that could dilute the integrity of elections.

      
📍
Your polling booth may change
During SIR, booth boundaries are sometimes reorganised. Always verify your assigned polling booth before election day.

      
⚠️
Missing names happen — act early
Names can be mistakenly deleted if BLOs cannot verify a resident (locked house, no response). If you moved temporarily during a SIR visit, check your name online and re-enrol if needed.
